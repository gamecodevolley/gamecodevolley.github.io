Does it work with iPhone?

No. Android 12 or newer only; tested with Samsung and Xiaomi.

Do I install anything on the phone?

No. It uses Android USB debugging, and the program guides you to turn it on.

USB or Wi-Fi?

USB is the most stable. Over Wi-Fi, the laptop and the phone go on the same network.

Which programs does it work with?

Any that use a Windows webcam, such as OBS and your scouting program.

Can I use one license on two laptops?

No. Each license is tied to one laptop.
